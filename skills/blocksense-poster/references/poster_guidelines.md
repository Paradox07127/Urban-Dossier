# Poster Text Guidelines

Rules for the LLM when generating the headline and summary text for BlockSense community posters.

## Headline

- Maximum 15 words
- Must mention the location name (address or neighborhood)
- Factual tone: state what the data shows, do not editorialize
- No exclamation marks
- No questions
- Examples:
  - "123 Main St, Brooklyn: Strong Transit, Emerging Safety Concerns"
  - "Prospect Heights Scores 72 — Good Access, Room to Improve"

## Summary

- Maximum 50 words
- Balance positive and negative findings from the data
- Lead with the strongest positive, then note the top concern
- Never invent numbers that are not present in the highlights data
- Use plain language — no jargon, no acronyms unless universally known (e.g., "NYC")
- Write for a general community audience, not data scientists
- One to two sentences is ideal
- Examples:
  - "This area offers excellent transit access with 2 subway stations and 156 street trees within walking distance. Traffic crashes are above the city median, and rodent activity warrants monitoring."
  - "A well-served neighborhood with strong dining options and good walkability. Building violations nearby suggest maintenance concerns worth tracking."

## General Rules

- Never fabricate statistics — every number must come from the highlights JSON
- Do not use superlatives ("best", "worst") unless the score is literally the highest or lowest possible
- Avoid fear-based language — frame concerns as "areas to watch" or "opportunities for improvement"
- Keep the tone informative and community-oriented
